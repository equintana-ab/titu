Frequently Asked Questions

Can I trade in a phone that has defects or damage?

Devices are evaluated at participating stores by our designated TITU point person. While only smartphones in good working condition are accepted, deductions to the final appraised value may be applied depending on any identified defects or cosmetic wear.

What brands and models are eligible for trade-in?

We accept major smartphone brands (select models only) in good working condition. You can trade in your old smartphone and trade up to any new mobile phone, tablet, or Macbook available at participating stores during the promo period.

Can I trade in more than one old phone to buy a single new device?

Yes! You can trade in multiple old smartphones to combine their appraised values toward the purchase of a single new mobile phone or tablet.

How is my old phone evaluated and appraised?

Simply surrender your smartphone to our store TITU point person. They will perform a step-by-step diagnostic test using the Compasia system. Once completed, the final appraised value and individual PDF receipt will be displayed directly on the mobile app.

How long is my device valuation receipt valid?

The final appraised value shown on your app-generated PDF receipt is valid for 24 hours only. Each traded-in device receives its own individual receipt.

How do store officers verify my trade-in valuation?

Before applying your discount at checkout, our Store Officers will validate your presented app receipt through the official Compasia application system.

How do you determine the trade-in value of my smartphone?

Your trade-in value is calculated through the official CompAsia valuation system, which assesses your device's model, physical condition, and functional health.

What documents do I need to bring for trade-in?

None! No valid IDs, receipts, or complex paperwork are required to submit your device for valuation.

Is my personal data safe when I trade in my old device?

Yes. All trade-in transactions and data handling are strictly managed in full compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012. We recommend performing a factory reset before surrendering your phone, but store officers also ensure proper device wipes.

How long does the entire trade-in and upgrade process take?

Timelines depend on the availability of your chosen trade-up unit:

  • In-Stock Store Units: Same-day processing and release.
  • Out-of-Stock Store Units: 3 to 5 business days for unit transfer.
  • Pre-Order Units: Delivery or pickup timing varies depending on the official launch/release date of the pre-ordered phone.

Are all Abenson stores participating in the Trade In, Trade Up program?

Most major Abenson flagship stores and Electroworld branches nationwide support in-store trade-in assessments. You can view the complete list of participating branches on our official Store Locator page.

How does the trade-in value affect the price of my new phone or tablet?

The net appraised value acts as a direct discount applied on top of the main unit price (SRP) of your chosen mobile device, tablet, or Macbook.

Is this promo compatible with all phones and pre-orders?

The trade up mechanic is applicable to all available phones and select pre-order units within the promo period.

Can I still avail of other store promos, freebies, or discounts on top of my trade-in discount?

Yes! You can still stack existing store promos (such as freebies, unit discounts, and add-ons) on top of your final trade-in valuation discount.

Can I cash out my trade-in amount or buy a phone with zero cash out?

Trade-in values and promo discounts are strictly non-convertible to cash. If your trade-in voucher covers 100% of the new phone's SRP, no extra payment is required.

What payment options are available for any remaining balance?

After your trade-in value is deducted from the new device's SRP, you can pay the remaining balance using any of the following accepted payment methods:

Straight Payment: Cash, Debit Card, Cash Card, or Credit Card.

Installment: 0% Credit Card Installment plan options.
